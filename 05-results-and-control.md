# 05 · Control — Results & Control Plan

## Results (3 months post-implementation)

| Metric | Before | After | Improvement | Target met? |
|--------|:------:|:-----:|:-----------:|:-----------:|
| Avg approval cycle time | 12 days | **5 days** | −58% | ✅ |
| On-time approval | 62% | **94%** | +32 pts | ✅ |
| Rework rate | 21% | **6%** | −15 pts | ✅ |
| Early-payment discounts captured | 18% | **71%** | +53 pts | ✅ |

## Control Plan (sustain the gains)

| What | How | Frequency | Owner |
|------|-----|-----------|-------|
| Cycle time & on-time % | Automated dashboard from workflow tool | Weekly | Finance Ops |
| Exception queue backlog | Monitor & clear | Daily | AP Team |
| SLA breaches | Auto-escalation + review | Real-time | Approvers |
| Process audit | Spot-check sample invoices | Monthly | BA / Internal Audit |

## Sustainability
- **Standard work** documented; new joiners trained on the workflow.
- **Control charts** monitor cycle time; investigate if it drifts above 6 days.
- **Continuous improvement:** quarterly review of exception reasons to remove remaining friction.

## Business Impact
Faster approvals restored vendor trust, recovered early-payment discounts (a direct cash saving), and freed AP staff from manual re-keying — a repeatable template for other finance processes.
