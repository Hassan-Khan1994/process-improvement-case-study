# 🔧 Process Improvement Case Study — Invoice Approval

A structured **process-improvement** portfolio piece using the **Lean Six Sigma DMAIC** method to cut the cycle time of an **invoice approval process** at a services company (*"Vantage Services"*, fictional) by **58%**.

> **Author:** Hassan Khan · Business Analyst / Process Improvement
> **Method:** DMAIC (Define · Measure · Analyze · Improve · Control)

---

## 🎯 The Problem
Invoices took an average of **12 days** to approve, causing late supplier payments, missed early-payment discounts, and strained vendor relationships. Target: reduce to **≤ 5 days**.

---

## 🗂️ DMAIC Deliverables

| Phase | Document | What it demonstrates |
|-------|----------|----------------------|
| **Define** | [Problem Statement & SIPOC](01-problem-statement.md) | Framing the problem, scope, goal |
| **Measure** | [Current-State Analysis](02-current-state-analysis.md) | Baseline metrics, as-is process |
| **Analyze** | [Root-Cause Analysis](03-root-cause-analysis.md) · [(Pareto CSV)](data/pareto-data.csv) | Fishbone, 5 Whys, Pareto |
| **Improve** | [Improvement Plan](04-improvement-plan.md) | To-be process & solutions |
| **Control** | [Results & Control Plan](05-results-and-control.md) | Sustained results & monitoring |

---

## 📊 Results

| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|:------:|:-----:|:-----------:|
| Avg approval cycle time | 12 days | 5 days | **−58%** |
| Invoices approved on time | 62% | 94% | **+32 pts** |
| Rework rate | 21% | 6% | **−15 pts** |
| Early-payment discounts captured | 18% | 71% | **+53 pts** |
